# Portfolio

## Projects

### Ngwazini WASH Project
This project was an independent study in the Duke department of Civil and Environmental Engineering where my fellow students and I designed a solar-powered WASH system to serve a primary school of ~100 students and staff facing water scarcity in Eswatini. The system integrated 2 solar panels, 20,000L water storage tanks, and 700W pump with water filtration and distribution infrastructure. The design and planning process happened over the Spring 2026 semester. As the quality control and safety manager, I designed and implemented the comprehensive construction site safety plan and performed quality control tasks on site. I was also actively involved in the solar system design and assmebly.

### Temperature Sensing Bot
This project was completed as a semester-long project for my ECE 110 course (Fundamentals of Electrical and Computer Engineering) earning my teammates and I the highest score in our lab. The challenge was to design a line-following bot that uses an RFID sensor to follow lines and an IR sensor to detect a cold object at any of the tick marks, then display a score on an LED monitor depending on whether a cold object was detected during the  run or not.

### Pulse Oximeter Using Arduino
This project was completed as a semester-long project for my EGR 105 course (Computing for Engineers) where my teammates and I designed a low-cost pulse oximeter and teacghing plan for DukeIgnite using an Arduino. As the team's Scrum Master, I managed our sprint cycles, delegated tasks, and collaborated with TAs and teaching staff to resolve technical blocks.
